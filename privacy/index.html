Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

1. Who we are

Gym OS is a product of Beyond Pixells. We provide gym management software (SaaS) to gym owners across India. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

2. Data we collect

  • Gym account data: name, email, phone, gym name and address — provided when you create an account.
  • Member data: names, phone numbers, emails, membership plans, attendance records and payment history — entered by you (the gym owner) or your staff.
  • Lead data: prospective member names, phone numbers, email addresses, source and follow-up status.
  • Payment data: transaction amounts, payment methods and invoice numbers. We do not store card numbers or UPI PINs — payments are processed by PCI-DSS compliant payment processors.
  • Usage data: login times, feature usage and device information — used to improve the product.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the Gym OS dashboard, QR check-in and related services.
  • To send WhatsApp reminders, renewal notifications and automated alerts on your behalf.
  • To generate analytics, revenue reports and business insights for your gym.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your queries.
  • To improve and develop features based on aggregate usage patterns.

4. Data storage and security

All data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Each gym's data is isolated per tenant at the database level. We do not store payment card data — all payments are handled by PCI-DSS compliant payment processors.

5. Data sharing

We never sell your data. Your gym's data belongs to you. We share data only with:

  • Payment processors — only payment-related data needed to process transactions.
  • WhatsApp/Meta — phone numbers and message content, to send messages you authorize.
  • Cloud hosting providers — infrastructure providers who store and transmit data.

We will only disclose data to law enforcement if legally required to do so by Indian law.

6. Your rights

  • Access: view all data stored about your gym at any time through the dashboard.
  • Correction: edit or delete any member, lead or payment record at any time.
  • Export: export your member and payment data as CSV at any time — free, forever.
  • Deletion: request complete deletion of your gym's data by contacting us. We will delete it within 30 days.

7. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, we retain data for 90 days (in case you reactivate), then permanently delete it.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email beyondpixells@gmail.com.
