Features

Everything Your Gym Needs — In One System

Replace your register, billing software, Instagram agency, and CRM with one dashboard

📊

Owner Dashboard

One screen shows everything — who's in the gym, today's revenue, new leads, expiring members, and what to do today. Works on any phone.

🌐

Premium Website

9+ pages, auto-synced with Gym OS. Change a price → website updates in 5 min. SEO-optimized, captures leads directly into your CRM.

📱

QR Check-In

Members scan QR → check-in in 2 seconds. Rotating QR (can't be screenshot). Works offline. Blocks expired members automatically.

💬

WhatsApp Automation

18 automated triggers: renewal reminders, birthday wishes, payment receipts, absent alerts. Max 1/day per member. No spam.

🎯

Lead CRM

All leads from Instagram, Google, website, WhatsApp, walk-ins — in one inbox. AI scores each lead hot/warm/cold. Never lose a lead.

💰

Payment Tracking

You control all money — cash, UPI, card. System just tracks. Auto-sends WhatsApp receipts. Revenue dashboard. Zero liability.

🏋️

Class Scheduling

Recurring classes, online booking, waitlist with auto-notify, live capacity tracking. Cancellation auto-notifies enrolled members.

👨‍🏫

Trainer Management

Trainer profiles, member assignment, schedules, commission tracking, payroll calculation. Trainer login with limited access.

🤖

AI Intelligence

Churn prediction: "Rahul hasn't come in 12 days — at risk." Revenue forecast. "What Should I Do Today" — 3 priority actions every morning.

🔥

Loyalty & Gamification

Points per check-in, streak tracking, challenges ("20 days in 30 = 1 month free"), achievement badges. Keeps members coming back.

📈

Reports & Analytics

Revenue, member growth, attendance trends, churn rate, lead conversion funnel, trainer performance. Export to PDF/Excel.

Built for India

Works offline. Works on ₹6,000 phones. Hindi + English. Festival-based offers. Low data usage. No payment gateway needed.
