FAQ

Questions Gym Owners Ask Us

Everything you need to know before booking a demo

What happens if my internet goes down?
QR check-in works offline. Members can scan and check in even with no internet. The system syncs automatically once connection is restored. All data is stored locally and uploaded when back online.
Do you handle our payments?
No. You control all money — cash, UPI, card, bank transfer. Gym OS only tracks payments and sends receipts. We never touch your funds. Zero liability on our end.
Is my gym's data safe?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Daily automatic backups. You own your data and can export it anytime as Excel or PDF. We never share or sell your data to third parties.
Is there a long-term contract?
No long-term lock-in. You pay month to month. Cancel anytime. The setup fee is one-time and covers website creation, data migration, and staff training.
Do I need any hardware?
No. You do not need any special hardware. Any phone or tablet can scan the QR code for check-in. No biometric machines, no RFID cards, no servers.
How long does setup take?
3 days from sign-up to fully running. Day 1: Website connected. Day 2: Members imported. Day 3: QR check-in and WhatsApp live, staff trained.
Can I customize the WhatsApp messages?
Yes. All 18 WhatsApp triggers can be customized with your gym's name, tone, and language. You can also disable any trigger you do not want to use.
