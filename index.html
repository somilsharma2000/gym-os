Straight answers

Can it really replace my register and Excel sheet?＋

Yes — that's the exact job it was built for. Members, plans, dues, renewals and check-ins all move into one system, and we import your existing member list for you during onboarding.

Does my staff need training to use it?＋

The daily surface is one screen: scan a QR, see green or red. Training takes one evening. The owner dashboard is equally simple on your phone.

Is the billing actually GST-compliant?＋

Invoices carry CGST/SGST computed at your gym's rate, sequential GST doc numbers, and a double-entry ledger behind every rupee — so your CA gets clean books, not screenshots.

What if the internet goes down?＋

Check-ins and door decisions are recorded locally-first in the app; data syncs when connectivity returns. Your door never stops working because Wi-Fi did.

How is this cheaper than Mindbody?＋

Mindbody charges in USD with per-staff add-ons — often ₹1 lakh+ a year for one gym. GYM OS is a flat monthly price in rupees, with every core feature included and no per-staff licence.

What does onboarding look like?＋

One day. Send your member list and plan details on WhatsApp; we import, print QR cards, and train your staff. You keep running the gym — that part doesn't change.
