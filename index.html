FAQ

Questions Club Owners Ask Us

Everything you need to know before booking a demo

Does 24/7 QR check-in work if the internet goes down?
Yes. QR check-in operates fully offline. Members scan and enter smoothly even without active internet. The system syncs data automatically as soon as connection is restored.
How does Gym OS handle 7-star luxury amenities like sauna, spa, and grooming stations?
Gym OS provides multi-zone access control. Members can book sauna, spa, or functional training sessions via WhatsApp or website, and staff can validate digital passes at grooming stations and locker rooms.
Do you process our membership payments directly?
No. You maintain full control over all money — cash, UPI, card, or bank transfer. Gym OS tracks payments, manages accounts, and auto-issues WhatsApp receipts with zero transaction fees.
Is our club and member data secure?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest with automated daily backups. You retain 100% ownership of your data and can export it anytime to Excel or PDF.
Is there a long-term contract lock-in?
No long-term contract required. You pay month-to-month and can cancel anytime. The one-time setup fee covers custom website setup, member migration, and staff training.
Do we need special hardware or biometric machines?
No special hardware required. Any smartphone or tablet can scan the QR code for instant check-in. No expensive biometric hardware, cards, or local servers required.
How long does full onboarding take?
3 days from sign-up to fully live. Day 1: Website connected. Day 2: Member records imported. Day 3: QR check-in and WhatsApp live, staff trained.
Can we customize the automated WhatsApp messages?
Yes. All 18 WhatsApp automation triggers can be tailored with your club's brand name, tone, and preferred language. You can also toggle specific triggers on or off anytime.
