Features

Everything Your Gym Needs — In One System

Replace your register, billing software, social media agency, and CRM with one dashboard

Owner Dashboard

One screen shows everything — who is in the gym, today's revenue, new leads, expiring members, and what to do today. Works on any phone.

Premium Website

9+ pages, auto-synced with Gym OS. Change a price and the website updates in 5 minutes. SEO-optimized, captures leads directly into your CRM.

QR Check-In

Members scan QR and check in in 2 seconds. Rotating QR prevents screenshots. Works offline. Blocks expired members automatically.

WhatsApp Automation

18 automated triggers: renewal reminders, birthday wishes, payment receipts, absent alerts. Maximum 1 per day per member. No spam.

Lead CRM

All leads from Instagram, Google, website, WhatsApp, walk-ins — in one inbox. AI scores each lead hot, warm, or cold. Never lose a lead.

Payment Tracking

You control all money — cash, card, bank transfer. The system just tracks. Auto-sends WhatsApp receipts. Revenue dashboard. Zero liability.

Class Scheduling

Recurring classes, online booking, waitlist with auto-notify, live capacity tracking. Cancellation auto-notifies enrolled members.

Trainer Management

Trainer profiles, member assignment, schedules, commission tracking, payroll calculation. Trainer login with limited access.

AI Intelligence

Churn prediction: alerts when a member has not visited in 12+ days. Revenue forecast. Three priority actions every morning.

Loyalty and Gamification

Points per check-in, streak tracking, challenges, achievement badges. Keeps members coming back and referring friends.

Reports and Analytics

Revenue, member growth, attendance trends, churn rate, lead conversion funnel, trainer performance. Export to PDF or Excel.

Built For Any Market

Works offline. Works on budget phones. Multi-language support. Festival-based offers. Low data usage. No payment gateway required.
