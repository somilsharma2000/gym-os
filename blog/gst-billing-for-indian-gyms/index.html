GST billing for Indian gyms: a practical guide

21 September 2026 · 6 min read · By the Gym OS team

Most gym software sold in India was designed for American tax law and retrofitted with a "tax field". Then your CA asks for GST-compliant invoices and the whole thing falls apart. This is a plain-language guide to what GST billing for a gym actually requires.

The three things a GST invoice must have

  • Your GSTIN and the customer's (if they have one) on the invoice.
  • CGST and SGST split correctly at your applicable rate — not one lump "tax" line.
  • A sequential, gap-free document numbering series. Gaps invite questions.

Where gyms go wrong

  • Cash payments recorded with no invoice at all — invisible revenue that becomes a liability.
  • The same payment entered twice by two different staff members at shift change.
  • Manual invoice numbers in Word templates — duplicates and gaps within a month.

What your CA needs from your software

Not screenshots. A clean ledger: every rupee in, tied to a member, with CGST/SGST computed per invoice and a report that reconciles payments to invoices without manual matching.

This is exactly why Gym OS builds invoices on top of a double-entry ledger. Every payment creates two balanced entries, duplicate recording is impossible by design, and your CA gets a ledger, not homework.
