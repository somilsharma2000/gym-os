Straight answers

Why is pricing annual?＋

Gym software works only when it stays switched on; annual keeps your price low and our support serious. You can walk away anytime — exports of your data are free, always.

Are there per-staff or per-member fees?＋

No. Ten staff or fifty, five hundred members or five thousand — the price is the price.

What about SMS and WhatsApp message costs?＋

WhatsApp automation runs on your gym's own WhatsApp Business number, so message costs stay at provider rates — we never mark them up.

Do you charge for onboarding?＋

No. Import, QR cards and staff training are included for every plan.
