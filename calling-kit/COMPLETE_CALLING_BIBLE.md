# SECTION 5: NEGOTIATION PLAYBOOK

### Rule 1: NEVER discount monthly. Only discount setup.

**Monthly ₹5,000 is non-negotiable.** This covers your real costs (server, WhatsApp API, support). If you discount this, you lose money every month.

**Setup is negotiable:**
- ₹30,000 → min ₹25,000 (if annual prepay)
- ₹20,000 → min ₹15,000 (if annual prepay)
- ₹15,000 → min ₹10,000 (if annual prepay or 2+ gyms)

### Rule 2: If they want a discount, trade for commitment.

**Never give a discount for free. Always get something in return.**

| They Want | You Get |
|---|---|
| "Reduce setup to ₹10K" | "Sure, if you commit to 12 months annual prepay" |
| "Reduce monthly to ₹3K" | "I can't reduce monthly, but I can give you 2 months free on annual (pay 10, get 12)" |
| "Give me a free trial" | "7-day free trial, but you pay full setup + first month after trial if you continue" |
| "I'll pay after I see results" | "I can't do that, but I offer 30-day money-back guarantee. If you don't see value in 30 days, full refund." |

### Rule 3: Price Anchoring Technique

**Always show the expensive option first:**

"Sir, we have 3 options:
- **Premium:** ₹30,000 setup — custom website, full system, on-site setup, 3 months premium support
- **Complete:** ₹20,000 setup — website, full system, remote setup, standard support
- **Standard:** ₹15,000 setup — template website, full system, self-setup

Which one interests you?"

**Psychology:** After hearing ₹30,000, the ₹15,000 feels cheap. Most will pick ₹20,000 (the middle option — "compromise effect").

### Rule 4: The "Cost Of Doing Nothing" Close

If they're hesitating, use this:

"Sir, I understand you want to think about it. But let me do some math with you:

- You're losing approximately ₹15,000/month in leaked revenue (expired members, missed renewals, lost leads)
- That's ₹500/day
- If you take 30 days to decide, that's ₹15,000 gone
- If you take 3 months, that's ₹45,000 gone

Our system costs ₹5,000/month. It would have paid for itself 3x in the time you spend 'thinking about it.'

I'm not trying to pressure you. I'm just showing you the math. Every day you wait costs you more than the system itself. Can I set this up for you this week?"

### Rule 5: The "Receptionist Comparison" Close

"Sir, your receptionist costs ₹[8,000-15,000]/month. She:
- Does check-in (our system does this automatically with QR)
- Reminds members about renewal (our system does this automatically on WhatsApp)
- Manages enquiries (our system captures and tracks all leads)
- Works 8-10 hours/day (our system works 24/7)
- Takes sick days (our system never does)
- Might leave for a better job (our system won't)

Our system does all of this for ₹5,000/month. You're not spending money — you're saving ₹3,000-10,000/month by replacing manual work with automation."

### Rule 6: The "Takeaway" Close (Reverse Psychology)

If they keep hesitating after 2-3 follow-ups:

"Sir, I can see this isn't the right time for you. That's completely fine. I'm going to stop calling because I don't want to waste your time or mine. I have 5 other gyms in [their city] to call this week.

But before I go — our current pricing is ₹[X] setup + ₹5,000/month. We're adding 20+ new features next quarter, and prices will go up for new clients. If you decide later, the price will be higher.

Here's my number: [your number]. Call me when you're ready. And if you want, I can send you a 2-minute video showing the system. WhatsApp number?"

**Psychology:** When you walk away, they often chase. "Wait, what features are coming? What's the new price going to be? Can you still do the old price?"
