# 1. PRICE FRAMING TECHNIQUES

## Technique 1: "Per Day" Reframe

**Don't say:** "₹5,000 per month"
**Say:** "₹167 per day — less than what one member pays you in a month"

**The script:**
"Sir, ₹5,000/month sounds like a number. Let me break it down. ₹5,000 per month = ₹167 per day. You charge one member ₹800-1,500 per month. This system costs less than what ONE member pays you. If it saves even ONE member from leaving, it pays for itself. Everything else is pure profit for you."

---

## Technique 2: "Already Paying" Reframe

**Don't say:** "You need to pay ₹5,000/month for our system"
**Say:** "You're ALREADY paying for it — in leaked revenue you can't see"

**The script:**
"Sir, here's the thing — you're ALREADY paying for a gym management system. You just don't know it. Every month, you're paying ₹15,000-25,000 in leaked revenue — expired members still attending, members who didn't renew because nobody reminded them, leads you forgot to follow up. That's your current 'system cost.' ₹25,000/month for a register book that loses your money.

Our system costs ₹5,000/month. It STOPS the ₹25,000 leak. So we're not ADDING a cost — we're REPLACING a bigger cost with a smaller one. You go from paying ₹25,000/month (in leaks) to paying ₹5,000/month (in software). Net savings: ₹20,000/month.

The system isn't an expense. It's a refund on money you're already losing."

---

## Technique 3: "Cost Comparison" Reframe

**Don't say:** "₹5,000/month for software"
**Say:** "Less than your electricity bill / less than 1/3rd of your receptionist"

- Your electricity bill: ₹8,000-15,000/month
- Your receptionist: ₹8,000-15,000/month
- Your Instagram management (if you hire someone): ₹3,000-10,000/month

Our system: ₹5,000/month. Less than your electricity. Less than 1/3rd of your receptionist. And it does the work of all of them — check-in, reminders, lead tracking, expense tracking. You're not adding a cost. You're replacing 4 costs with 1."

# 2. THE "GOOD-BETTER-BEST" DECOY PRICING

## How To Present 3 Options So They Always Pick The Middle

**Never show just one price. Always show 3. The middle one is your target.**

**The script:**
"Sir, we have 3 packages. Let me show you all 3 so you can decide what's right for your gym:

**OPTION A — STARTER (₹15,000 setup + ₹5,000/month)**
- Template website (5 pages, basic design)
- Gym OS core (members, check-in, payments, basic WhatsApp)
- Email support (48-hour response)
- Remote setup
- 1 staff login

**OPTION B — COMPLETE (₹20,000 setup + ₹5,000/month) ← MOST POPULAR**
- Custom website (9+ pages, premium design, SEO, Instagram embed, WhatsApp widget)
- Full Gym OS (everything in Starter + lead CRM, class scheduling, trainer management, all 18 WhatsApp automations, AI insights, reports)
- WhatsApp + Instagram + Google integration
- On-site or video setup + staff training
- 3 staff logins
- Priority support (24-hour response)
- Monthly system health check

**OPTION C — PREMIUM (₹30,000 setup + ₹5,000/month)**
- Everything in Complete +
- Fully custom website with premium branding
- AI intelligence suite (churn prediction, revenue forecasting, dynamic pricing, daily action suggestions)
- Full 20 automation templates (win-back, at-risk save, NPS, Google review automation)
- Loyalty & gamification system (points, challenges, badges)
- Expense & P&L tracking
- Progress tracking with charts
- 5 staff logins
- Dedicated WhatsApp support (2-hour response)
- Quarterly review call
- Early access to new features

Which one sounds right for your gym?"

### The Psychology:
- **Option A (₹15K)** exists to make Option B look like a better deal. It's intentionally limited — "template website," "basic," "email support only," "1 login." Most owners will think "I need more than basic."
- **Option B (₹20K)** is your TARGET. It says "MOST POPULAR" — social proof. It has everything they need. The jump from ₹15K to ₹20K is only ₹5,000 but adds a LOT more value.
- **Option C (₹30K)** exists to make Option B look affordable. It's premium and expensive. Most owners will think "₹30K is too much, but ₹20K gives me almost everything."

**Result:** 60-70% will pick Option B (₹20K). 20% will pick Option C (₹30K). 10% will pick Option A (₹15K). Nobody feels pressured — they chose.

### What If They Ask "What's The Difference Between B And C?"

"Sir, the main differences:
- Option B gives you everything you need to run your gym digitally TODAY — website, members, check-in, WhatsApp, leads, classes, trainers.
- Option C adds the BRAIN — AI that predicts which members will leave, tells you what to do every morning, tracks expenses, gives you P&L, and includes loyalty programs that keep members longer.

Think of it this way: Option B is your management system. Option C is your management system + a business advisor that never sleeps.

If your gym has 200+ members, I'd recommend Option C — the AI pays for itself by saving 2-3 members from churning every month. If you're under 200, Option B is perfect — you can upgrade to C anytime."

### Your Risk:

- Maybe 30-40% won't convert (you lose setup time)
- 60-70% WILL convert because they've experienced it
- The 60-70% who convert would NEVER have converted from a cold pitch alone

# 13. THE "PAYMENT TIMING" PSYCHOLOGY

## When You Ask For Money Matters

### Bad timing: "₹30,000 setup + ₹5,000/month" in first 2 minutes of call
### Good timing: Build value for 10 minutes → show ROI → THEN reveal price

### The sequence:
1. **Minutes 0-2:** Hook + qualify (no price mention)
2. **Minutes 2-5:** Show pain (leaked revenue, lost members, missed leads)
3. **Minutes 5-8:** Show solution (QR, WhatsApp, leads, AI, website)
4. **Minutes 8-10:** Show ROI (₹47,000 found, 35% renewals, 22 leads)
5. **Minute 10:** Reveal price (₹30K + ₹5K/month)
6. **Minute 11:** Immediately reframe ("less than 1 member's monthly fee")
7. **Minute 12:** Offer demo/trial (risk reversal)
8. **Minute 13:** Close ("Can I set this up this week?")

### The Rule:
**Never reveal price before showing value.** If they ask price early: "Sir, let me show you what you're getting first, then we'll talk price. If it's not worth it, I won't waste your time. Fair?"

# 14. THE "ROUND NUMBER" PREMIUM SIGNAL

## ₹5,000/month Feels More Premium Than ₹4,999/month

**Keep prices round.** Round numbers signal confidence and premium quality.

- ✅ ₹5,000/month (confident, premium, no tricks)
- ✅ ₹30,000 setup (clean, confident)
- ✅ ₹15,000 / ₹20,000 / ₹30,000 (clean tiers)
- ❌ ₹4,999/month (feels like a marketing trick, cheap)
- ❌ ₹29,999 setup (feels like e-commerce discount, not B2B)
- ❌ ₹14,999 (feels like they're being manipulated)

**Gym owners are smart. They see ₹4,999 and think "they think I'm stupid enough to believe this is ₹4,000." ₹5,000 says "this is what it costs, and it's worth it."**

**Step 7: Close**
"Which package feels right for your gym? Or can I show you a 15-minute demo first so you can see exactly what you're getting?"
