## THE EXACT PROMPT TO FIND QUALITY GYMS

### GOOGLE MAPS SEARCH PROMPT:

```
"gyms in [CITY NAME]" on Google Maps

FILTER BY:
- Rating: 4.0+ stars (shows they care about quality)
- Reviews: 50+ minimum (shows they have enough members)
- Price: ₹500+ per month membership (if listed)
- Photos: 10+ photos (owner cares about branding)
- Website: Has NO website or has a BAD website (outdated, broken, not mobile-friendly)
- Google Business Profile: Claimed and active (shows owner is tech-aware)
- Hours: Opens before 6 AM (serious gym, not casual)
```

### INSTAGRAM SEARCH PROMPT:

```
Search: "[CITY NAME] gym" on Instagram

QUALIFY BY:
- 1,000+ followers (established gym, enough members)
- Posts regularly (at least 1 post per week — owner is active)
- Has Instagram Business account (not personal — owner is business-minded)
- Stories active (owner uses Instagram for marketing)
- DMs open (we can reach them)
- Bio has phone number or WhatsApp (direct contact)
- Bio says "DM for membership" or "enroll now" (they're actively selling)

SKIP:
- Personal trainer accounts (not a gym)
- Fitness influencer accounts (not a gym)
- Gym chains with 5+ branches (too big, corporate decision, our system can't serve them)
- Accounts with 0 posts in last 30 days (owner is inactive)
```

### JUSTDIAL / INDIAMART SEARCH PROMPT:

```
Search: "gym" in [CITY NAME] on JustDial

QUALIFY BY:
- 4+ star rating
- 20+ reviews/ratings
- "Verified" listing
- Has phone number listed
- Description mentions "personal training", "group classes", "multiple plans" (shows they have services to manage)

SKIP:
- 1-2 star ratings
- Less than 10 reviews (too new or too small)
- "Home gym" or "personal training at home" (not a gym)
```

## THE 7-POINT QUALITY SCORING SYSTEM

Score each gym out of 100. Only call gyms scoring 60+.

| Criteria | Points | How to Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Member count (estimated) | 25 | Reviews on Google = member count ÷ 5. 50 reviews ≈ 250 members. 100 reviews ≈ 500 members. Need 30+ reviews = 150+ members. |
| 2. Google rating | 15 | 4.5+ stars = 15 pts. 4.0-4.4 = 10 pts. Below 4.0 = 0 pts (skip). |
| 3. Website status | 20 | No website = 20 pts (perfect target). Bad/outdated website = 15 pts. Good website = 5 pts. Great website = 0 pts (skip — they already have one). |
| 4. Instagram presence | 15 | 1,000+ followers + active = 15 pts. 500-1,000 + active = 10 pts. Under 500 or inactive = 5 pts. No Instagram = 0 pts (might not be tech-ready). |
| 5. Premium signals | 10 | Offers personal training, group classes, multiple plans, has AC, has shower, has parking, has diet consultation. 3+ of these = 10 pts. |
| 6. Location wealth indicator | 10 | Located in premium residential area, near corporate offices, near college with rich students = 10 pts. Market area = 5 pts. Low-income area = 0 pts (can't afford ₹30K). |
| 7. Owner tech-readiness | 5 | Responds to Google reviews (shows they use phone for business), has WhatsApp Business number listed, Instagram is Business account = 5 pts. |

| Answer | Action |
|---|---|
| Yes | ✅ Good — they have premium services, can afford our system |
| No | ⚠️ Might be basic gym — proceed cautiously |

| "Hum manage kar lete hain" | ⚠️ Might not feel the pain — harder to sell |
