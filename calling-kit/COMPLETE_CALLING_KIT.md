## 1. WHAT IS GYM OS — QUICK SUMMARY

**One line:** "Gym OS is a complete gym management system — website, member management, WhatsApp automation, lead CRM, and AI insights — all in one."

**The problem:** Most Indian gyms run on a register book and a basic billing app. They lose members because they forget to remind them about renewal. They lose leads because nobody follows up. They lose money because expired members keep coming for free. They have no website or a bad one.

**The solution:** Gym OS handles the full lifecycle — visitor becomes a lead, lead becomes a trial, trial becomes a member, member stays because of automated reminders, member refers friends, and the owner knows exactly what's happening through the dashboard.

**What makes it different:**
- Built for Indian gyms (works on ₹6,000 phones, Hindi support, offline mode, festival offers)
- Website + Management system in one (not separate)
- WhatsApp automation built-in (20+ auto-triggers)
- AI that tells the owner "what to do today"
- Lead CRM with 13 sources in one inbox (Instagram, Google, website, WhatsApp, walk-in)
- No payment gateway — owner keeps full control of money

## 3. THE PITCH SCRIPT (Full Call Flow)

### Step 1: Opening (15 seconds)

"Hello, am I speaking with the owner of {Gym Name}?"

*(If no → "When would be a good time to reach them? Thank you." → Hang up. Note timing.)*

*(If yes → continue)*

"Hi, my name is {Your Name}. I'm calling from Gym OS. We build complete gym management systems — website, member management, WhatsApp automation, lead CRM — all in one platform.

I'm not calling to sell you anything right now. I just want to show you something that could save you 2-3 hours every day and help you keep more members.

**Do you have 2 minutes?**"

*(If "I'm busy" → use Objection #1)*
*(If "Yes" → continue)*
*(If "No" → "No problem. Can I send you a short video on WhatsApp? It's 2 minutes. You can watch when you're free. What's your WhatsApp number?" → Send video, follow up in 3 days)*

### Step 2: The Problem (30 seconds)

"I'll keep it very short. Most gym owners I talk to have 3 problems:

**Number 1:** Members forget to renew. They don't come for 2 weeks, and nobody calls them. By the time you notice, they've joined another gym.

**Number 2:** Leads come from Instagram, Google, walk-ins — but there's no system to track them. You get 10 inquiries, follow up with 3, and forget the other 7.

**Number 3:** Expired members keep coming. Your staff doesn't check, and you're losing ₹15,000-20,000 every month in free gym access.

Does any of this sound familiar?"

*(Pause. Let them answer. If they say yes to any → continue)*

### Step 3: The Solution (60 seconds)

"Great. So Gym OS solves all 3.

**For renewals:** Our system automatically sends WhatsApp reminders 7 days, 3 days, and 1 day before a member's membership expires. No more forgotten renewals. Owners using our system see 15-30% more renewals just from this.

**For leads:** Every lead from Instagram, Google, website, WhatsApp, walk-in — all come into one inbox. The system scores them — hot, warm, cold — so you know who to call first. You never lose a lead again.

**For expired members:** The system flags every expired member who's still coming to your gym. You see the list, you know exactly how much money you're losing, and you can fix it.

Plus, you get a **complete website** — your pricing, class schedule, trainer profiles — all auto-synced. If you change a price in the system, your website updates automatically in 5 minutes.

And the system tells you every morning: **'Here's what you should do today.'** Call these 3 leads. These 5 members are about to leave. This member expired but came yesterday."

### Step 4: The Demo Offer (15 seconds)

"I don't want to take more of your time on the phone. **Can I come to your gym and show you the system in 10 minutes?** I'll show you live on my phone — how QR check-in works, how WhatsApp automation works, how the dashboard looks.

No cost, no commitment. Just 10 minutes. If you like it, we talk further. If not, no problem.

**What day works for you — this week or next week?**"

*(If they agree → book demo, note date/time)*
*(If "I'll think about it" → use Objection #3)*
*(If "How much does it cost?" → use Objection #2)*
*(If "I don't need this" → use Objection #4)*

### Step 5: Close / Next Step

**If demo booked:**
"Perfect. I'll come on {day} at {time}. I'll show you everything on my phone — 10 minutes. You'll see exactly how it works for your gym. See you then!"

**If follow-up needed:**
"Totally understand. Let me send you a 2-minute video on WhatsApp that shows the system. You can watch it whenever you're free, and I'll call you next week to see what you think. What's your WhatsApp number?"

## 4. OBJECTION HANDLING (12 Objections)

### Objection 1: "I'm busy right now"

"I understand completely — running a gym is a full-time job. That's exactly why I'm calling. Our system saves gym owners 2-3 hours every day by automating WhatsApp reminders, member tracking, and lead follow-ups.

Can I send you a 2-minute video on WhatsApp? You watch it whenever you're free — maybe tonight after the gym closes. I'll call you back next week.

**What's your WhatsApp number?**"

### Objection 2: "How much does it cost?"

"Great question. We have 3 plans:

**Starter: ₹799/month** — for small gyms under 100 members. Member management, QR check-in, basic WhatsApp automation.

**Professional: ₹1,499/month** — this is what most gyms choose. Everything in Starter plus full lead CRM, class scheduling, trainer management, advanced WhatsApp automation.

**Premium: ₹2,999/month** — for larger gyms. Everything plus AI insights, full automation suite, advanced analytics.

There's also a **one-time website setup fee of ₹3,000-₹7,000** depending on how custom you want it, plus ₹500-1,000/month for website hosting and maintenance.

But honestly, don't decide based on price right now. **Let me show you the system first.** If you like it, we'll figure out the right plan for your gym. If it saves you even 5 members a month from not renewing, the system pays for itself.

**Can I come show you in 10 minutes?**"

### Objection 3: "I'll think about it"

"Of course. But can I ask — what specifically would you like to think about?

Is it the price, or whether you need it, or something else?"

*(Listen. Then address the specific concern.)*

"Here's what I suggest. Let me come show you the system for 10 minutes. You'll see exactly how it works for YOUR gym. After that, take all the time you need to decide. But at least you'll know what you're deciding about.

**What day works — Thursday or Friday?**"

### Objection 4: "I don't need this / My register works fine"

"I'm sure it does — you've been running successfully without software. But here's the thing: a register doesn't remind you when a member is about to expire. A register doesn't capture the Instagram lead that messaged you at 11 PM. A register doesn't tell you that 5 expired members came to your gym last week for free.

You're probably losing ₹15,000-20,000 every month in expired members attending, uncollected renewals, and lost leads — without even knowing it.

Our system shows you exactly where the money is leaking. **Can I show you in 10 minutes how much you might be losing?**"

### Objection 5: "I already have software"

"That's great — which one are you using?"

*(Listen. Most Indian gym software is basic billing + member entry.)*

"Most gym software I've seen does billing and member entries. But does yours:

- Automatically send WhatsApp reminders for renewals?
- Capture leads from Instagram and Google in one inbox?
- Tell you which expired members are still coming?
- Give you a website that auto-syncs with your system?
- Tell you 'what to do today' every morning?

If it does all that, you're in great shape. But if any of those are missing, it's worth seeing what Gym OS does differently. **Can I show you in 10 minutes?**"

### Objection 6: "I don't have time to learn software"

"Totally understand. That's why our system is designed for gym owners, not tech people. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Gym OS.

The setup takes 30 minutes — we do it for you. After that, the system runs on autopilot. You just see a dashboard every morning that tells you what to do.

You don't need to learn anything. **The system works for you, not the other way around.**

Can I come show you how simple it is? 10 minutes."

### Objection 7: "My staff won't use it"

"We've designed it so that staff only see what they need. A trainer sees their members and schedule. A receptionist sees check-in and lead entry. They don't see your financial data.

QR check-in is faster than writing in a register — they'll actually prefer it. And the system works offline too, so if internet is down, check-in still works.

**Let me show you how simple it is for staff. 10 minutes.**"

### Objection 8: "Send me details on WhatsApp"

"Absolutely. But can I ask — do you prefer a video or a PDF?

Let me send you a 2-minute video showing the system in action. And I'll also send our pricing details.

**What's your WhatsApp number?**

I'll send it right now. Can I call you back on {specific day} to hear your thoughts?"

*(ALWAYS book a follow-up call. Never just send and hope.)*

### Objection 9: "I'll talk to my partner/co-owner"

"That makes sense. Decisions like this should be discussed together. When are you both at the gym together?

How about I come when you're both there? I'll show you the system in 10 minutes, and you can discuss it together. That way you both see it at the same time.

**What day are you both there?**"

### Objection 10: "Your price is too high"

"I understand. But let me ask — how much do you lose every month in expired members who keep coming? In members who don't renew because nobody reminded them? In leads you forgot to follow up with?

Most gym owners we've worked with were losing ₹15,000-30,000 every month without knowing it. The system costs ₹1,499/month. If it saves you even 2 members from leaving, it's already paid for.

Plus, the website alone costs ₹3,000-7,000 to set up. Most developers charge ₹15,000-25,000 for a website without any management system.

**Let me show you the system first, then we can talk about the right plan for your budget. Can I come for 10 minutes?**"

### Objection 11: "I don't trust online systems with my data"

"Completely understandable. Your members' data is sensitive. Here's how we handle it:

- Only you and your authorized staff can see the data
- We don't store any government IDs (no Aadhaar, no PAN)
- Progress photos are permission-based — members choose who can see them
- Only you can export data, staff can request but you approve
- Everything is backed up daily

Your data is YOUR data. We just provide the tool to manage it.

**Can I show you the security features in a 10-minute demo?**"

### Objection 12: "Just send me the brochure"

"I'll send you a summary right now on WhatsApp. But honestly, a brochure doesn't show you how the system feels. It's like reading about a car vs test-driving it.

Let me send you a 2-minute video AND come show you in person. 10 minutes, no pressure. You'll see QR check-in, WhatsApp automation, the dashboard — all live.

**What's your WhatsApp number, and what day works for a quick demo?**"

## 6. CALLING GUIDELINES & DAILY TARGETS

### DAILY TARGETS
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Calls per day | 40-50 |
| Conversations (owner picks up) | 15-20 |
| Demos booked | 3-5 |
| WhatsApp follow-ups sent | 10 |
| Notes updated in tracker | ALL calls |

### CALLING RULES
1. **Call between 11 AM - 1 PM and 4 PM - 7 PM** (avoid early morning — owners are busy with morning crowd, avoid 1-4 PM — owner may be resting)
2. **Never call before 10 AM or after 8 PM**
3. **Sunday is for follow-ups only** (don't cold call on Sundays)
4. **Smile while talking** — it changes your tone
5. **Stand up while calling** — more energy in your voice
6. **Use the owner's name** at least 3 times in the call
7. **Always book a specific day and time** for the demo — never "sometime next week"
8. **Always get WhatsApp number** — even if they say no, you can send a video and follow up
9. **Never argue** — if they say no, thank them and move on
10. **Update tracker after EVERY call** — even if they didn't pick up

### DEMO RULES
1. **Demo should be 10-15 minutes max**
2. **Show on your phone/laptop — live system**
3. **Show these 5 things in this order:**
   - QR check-in (30 seconds — "Watch this, member scans, done. No register needed.")
   - WhatsApp automation (2 minutes — "See, member expires in 7 days, system auto-sends this message.")
   - Revenue dashboard (2 minutes — "See today's revenue, this month's revenue, outstanding dues.")
   - Revenue leak detection (2 minutes — "See this? 3 expired members came this week. That's ₹X you're losing.")
   - AI "What Should I Do Today" (1 minute — "Every morning the system tells you: call these leads, these members are at risk.")
4. **Don't show more than 5 things** — they'll get overwhelmed
5. **Ask after each feature:** "Does this make sense? Can you see how this helps?"
6. **Close with:** "What do you think? Should we set this up for your gym?"

### FOLLOW-UP RULES
1. **After sending video on WhatsApp → call back in 3 days**
2. **After demo → call next day to close**
3. **If "thinking about it" → call in 5 days**
4. **If "talk to partner" → call in 3 days, ask when partner is available**
5. **If no answer 3 times → mark as "unreachable" in tracker, move on**
6. **Never call more than 5 times** to the same gym — you're losing other opportunities

## 8. DATA EXTRACTION GUIDE (How To Find Quality Gyms)

### STEP 1: Choose Your Area
- You're assigned a specific city/area (e.g., Ujjain, Indore, Bhopal)
- Focus ONLY on your area — you know it better than anyone
- Don't extract data from other areas

### STEP 2: Open Google Maps
- Go to google.com/maps
- Search: "gym in {your area}"
- Example: "gym in Ujjain" or "gym in Freeganj Ujjain"

### STEP 3: Filter & Score
For each gym that shows up on Google Maps, check:

| Check | Where | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Has 50+ Google reviews? | Google Maps listing | +1 |
| Rating 4.0+? | Google Maps listing | +1 |
| Has website link? | Google Maps listing (click website) | +1 |
| Website is outdated/empty? | Visit the website | +2 |
| Has Instagram link? | Google Maps or search Instagram | +1 |
| Instagram has 500+ followers? | Instagram profile | +1 |
| Multiple locations? (chain) | Google Maps | -5 (skip) |
| Premium/gold's gym? | Name/branding | -5 (skip) |
| Open 1+ year? | Google Maps "opened" or reviews history | +1 |
| Phone number available? | Google Maps listing | +1 (required) |

**Score 7+ → Add to your list (priority)**
**Score 4-6 → Add to your list (standard)**
**Score < 4 → Skip**

### STEP 4: Build Your List
Create an Excel sheet with these columns:

| Gym Name | Area | Phone | Owner Name (if found) | Score | Has Website? | Has Instagram? | Review Count | Rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### STEP 5: Find More Gyms (Beyond Google Maps)
- Search on Instagram: #gym{yourcity} (e.g., #gymujjain)
- Search on JustDial: "gym in {your area}"
- Ask local people: "Which gyms are popular here?"
- Walk around your area — notice gym signboards
- Check Facebook: search "gym in {your area}"

### STEP 6: Prioritize
- Sort by score (highest first)
- Start calling from the top
- Call all 7+ scored gyms first
- Then 4-6 scored gyms
- Skip anything below 4

### QUALITY OVER QUANTITY
- 50 high-quality gyms (scored 7+) > 200 random gyms
- Better to call 40 gyms you've researched than 100 you haven't
- Every call should be to a gym that could actually buy
